Common questions

Our team replies within the hour on business days. Contact us

How fast will I get a quote?

Most quotes go out within 2 business hours. Oversized, multi-stop or special cargo shipments can take up to 24 hours because we confirm space with the carrier first.

Where do I find my tracking number?

It is in your booking confirmation email. It starts with MGT followed by the year and order number — for example MGT-2026-001. Enter it on the tracking page.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Our licensed brokers file the import and export paperwork, classify your goods and pay duties on your behalf so the shipment is not held.

Is my cargo insured?

Carriers only pay a small amount per kilogram if something goes wrong. Our cargo insurance covers the full declared value of your goods for loss and damage. Add it when you request a quote.
